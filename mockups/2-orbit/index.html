Music production, East Village

Walk in with a hum. Walk out with a song.

The studio has red walls, warm wood, and a desk full of vintage gear. Jay Critch, Ron Suno, Ruben Blades and Making Movies have all recorded in this room. Alex shows you how the pieces fit, then hands you the controls.

  1. Ring the buzzer. Alex brings you upstairs and shows you around the studio.
  2. Build a beat. Drums, bass and chords, starting from silence.
  3. Step into the booth. Sing, rap, talk or laugh. It all counts.
  4. Take it home. You leave with the track you made.
  • About 2 hours
  • From $150 per guest
  • Ages 10 and up

Learn how DJs keep a room moving. Alex walks you through two songs becoming one: finding the beat, lining it up, and easing from one track into the next. By the end you play a short set of your own, in any genre you like.

Ask about a private session

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Email us and Alex will reply.

I've never touched music gear. Is that okay?

Yes, completely. Most guests are beginners. Alex explains everything as you go, and you'll be making sound within the first few minutes.

Do I get to keep what we make?

Yes. After a production session you leave with your track. If you'd like, ask Alex about recording your DJ set too.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Every studio has all the gear you'll need. For meditation, wear something comfortable.

Can kids come?

Production sessions are open to ages 10 and up. Age limits for the DJ and meditation rooms will be added here soon.

What if my plans change?

Cancel at least one day before your start time and you'll get a full refund.

Is it in English or Spanish?

Either one. Pick whichever you're more comfortable in.
