Before you come

I have zero music experience. Is that a problem?

It's the most common way people arrive. Alex starts from nothing and moves at your speed. If you can tap a rhythm on a table, you can make a beat.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

Cancel up to a day before your start time for a full refund. To move to another time, cancel and rebook, or reply to your confirmation email.

How many people can come?

Book up to 6 guests online. For larger groups, ask about a private booking and we'll work out the room and the plan. Placeholder

Do I need to bring anything?

No. All the gear is in the room. Bring headphones if you like your own, and a song or two you love for inspiration.

Where exactly are the studios?

The beat-making studio is in the East Village. The other rooms are in Manhattan. The exact address and buzzer instructions come with your confirmation.
