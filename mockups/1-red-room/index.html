What two hours in the beat room looks like

  1. Ring the buzzer

    I'll come down, bring you up the stairs and get you settled on the couch.

  2. Take the tour

    The desk, the vintage gear, the vocal booth, and a few stories about who's sat where you're sitting.

  3. Build the beat

    Drums first, then bass and chords. You make the calls; I handle the buttons you haven't met yet.

  4. Step up to the mic

    Sing, rap, talk, laugh. Whatever goes in the booth goes on the track.

  5. Leave with your song

    We do a quick mix together and I send you the finished track to keep.

Ask about a private session

Before you come up

Still wondering? Text me and I'll answer between sessions.

I've never made music. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most guests are total beginners, and the sessions are built for that. If you can nod your head to a song, you're qualified.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself. Every room has all the gear you'll need. If you have songs you love, bring a list; we can use them as a starting point.

How many people can come?

Sessions work best for small groups. For bigger groups or a private booking, use the private session link above and I'll sort it out.

Do I get to keep what we make?

Yes. In the beat room you leave with your finished track. I'll send it over after the session.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least one day before your start time and you'll get a full refund.

How do I find the studio?

You'll get the exact address and directions with your booking confirmation. When you arrive, press the buzzer and I'll come down to get you.
