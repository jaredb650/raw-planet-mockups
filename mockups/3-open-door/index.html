Good questions

Do I need any musical experience?

None at all. Most guests have never made music before. Sessions start from zero and move at your pace.

What do I take home?

From the music production session, you leave with the track you made together. We'll send you the file after your visit.

How many people can come?

Book for yourself or a small group. For bigger groups or private events, get in touch and we'll plan it with you.

Can kids join?

Music production is open to ages 10 and up. Ages for the DJ and meditation sessions are still to be confirmed.

What if my plans change?

Cancel at least 1 day before your start time for a full refund.

How do I find the studio?

Each experience has its own studio, and you'll get the exact address and directions by email after you book. At the East Village studio, press the buzzer and Alex will bring you upstairs.

Are sessions in Spanish?

Yes. Alex hosts in English and Spanish, so just let us know when you book.
